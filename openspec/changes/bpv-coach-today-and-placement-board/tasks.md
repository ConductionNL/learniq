# Tasks: bpv-coach-today-and-placement-board

- [x] **T1**: register: `bpv-placement.phase`, `phaseDueAt`, `lateLabel` (calculation); the phase derivation on every fact change; manual move log
  - As built: `lib/Bpv/PlacementPhase.php` derives the phase, `phaseDueAt` and `lateLabel` ("2 weken te laat" from a week on, "te laat sinds 1 oktober" before); `BpvPlacementPhaseStamp` writes them on every placement save, and `BpvVisitPhaseRollup` saves the placement again when one of its visits changes. A manual move is `phaseOverride: {phase}`: the server adds who and when, appends it to `phaseMoves`, and drops it at the next fact change. `lateLabel` is written on save, not a live calculation: a placement nobody touches keeps its label until its next save (a daily refresh is not built). A POK-active placement before its period starts counts as running.
- [x] **T2**: register: `bpv-visit` with lifecycle; link from `bpv-visit-report`
- [x] **T3**: propose a visit date; the praktijkopleider accepts or answers in her portal (trainer audience action)
  - As built: the coach proposes by filling in `proposedAt` on the visit (the new "Company visits" list, `/bpv/visits`); `BpvVisitProposalStamp` then puts an unplanned or proposed visit in `proposed` and clears an earlier answer and planned moment. The praktijkopleider sees the proposed visits of her own placements on her portal (`poBpvVisits`, a "Visit dates to agree" block on her overview) and answers through learniq's endpoint (`answerBpvVisit` → `POST /api/portal/bpv-visits/answer`, `PortalVisitAnswer`): accept plans the visit for the proposed moment, answer sends it back unplanned with her note. A planned, held or cancelled visit keeps its moment.
- [x] **T4**: the placement board per phase (library board view, card with late line)
  - As built: "Placements by phase" (`/bpv/board`) is the library index page in board mode over `bpv-placement`, one column per `phase` (labels from the schema's `enumLabels`; BpvPlacement 0.7.0, register 0.51.0). A card reads the student, the training company and the late line in words (`lateLabel`), and `phaseDueAt` marks it overdue or due soon. A drag never writes `phase`: the `movePlacementPhase` handler sends `phaseOverride: {phase}`, the manual move T1 logs and holds until the next fact change.
