- [x] **T4**: `requestProofOfEnrolment` through filinq's render endpoint
  - Built 2026-11-17: the student action `requestProofOfEnrolment` (no fields; the learner comes from the claim) forwards to `POST /api/portal/proof-of-enrolment` (`PortalProofOfEnrolmentController`, the check-in receiver's admit-and-resolve flow). `ProofOfEnrolment` takes her active enrolment, renders it through filinq's `api/v1/documents/render` with template `proof-of-enrolment` (the report card's endpoint and credential), and keeps the PDF on the enrolment as "Bewijs van inschrijving, schooljaar 2026-2027" (one per school year, replaced on a new request); `LearnerDocuments` lists the enrolment's files under "Choices and statements".
