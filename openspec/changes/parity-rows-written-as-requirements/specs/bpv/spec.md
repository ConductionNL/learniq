## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The workplace supervisor is kept on record as a person

A `Praktijkopleider` MUST record the supervisor's given and family name, email, phone, training company name and Chamber of Commerce (KvK) number, and whether they are active. A placement MUST name its supervisor. The BPV staff groups MUST be able to read supervisors, and the other groups MUST NOT.

#### Scenario: A coordinator records a supervisor

- **GIVEN** a work placement at "Bakkerij De Korenschoof"
- **WHEN** a coordinator adds the supervisor Anja Visser with her email and the company's KvK number, and links her to the placement
- **THEN** the placement page shows Anja Visser as the supervisor
