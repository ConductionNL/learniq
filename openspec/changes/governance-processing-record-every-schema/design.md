# Design: keep the GDPR record of processing up to date by itself

## Context

At development `24b9ae95`:

- 15 schemas carry `x-openregister-processing`: `Credential`, `AiFeature`, `Attestation`, `ExternalTrainingRecord`, `LearnerProfile`, `RolloverPlan`, `Assessment`, `ExemptionCase`, `FraudCase`, `DossierNote`, `BehaviourIncident`, `WellbeingCheckIn`, `AttendanceRecord`, `FirstAidIncident`, `DossierReview`.
- The block holds `code`, `naam`, `doelbinding`, `rechtsgrond`, `dataCategories`, `backend`, `retentionReference`, `logReads`, `reviewIntervalMonths`, `subjectIdFields`.
- 57 schemas have a field that names or points at a person (`learnerId`, `learnerRef`, `ncUserId`, `teacherIds`, `managerId`, `guardian*`, `parentIds`, `employeeId`, `markedBy` and similar) and no block. The list is in the proposal's evidence and is regenerated by the check in task 3.1.
- openregister `openspec/changes/processing-activity-register`: the platform record, built from these blocks.

## Screen

No board. A schema annotation has no screen of its own; the record shows on the privacy pages, which belong to the privacy officer and are not drawn on the canvas (`5NkFW28vZUUij43xzxHg5a`; `capabilities-learniq.md` section 4).

## Decisions

### D1: A few activities, many schemas

The record is read by a person. Fifty-seven activities, one per schema, would bury the purposes. Schemas are grouped by purpose:

| Activity code | Purpose | Schemas (examples) |
|---|---|---|
| `learniq-teaching-and-enrolment` | Deliver education and training | Enrolment, Cohort, LessonCompletion, ElectiveSignUp, Application, SubjectChoice |
| `learniq-assessment-and-results` | Assess and record results | Submission, SubmissionMark, AssessmentResult, GradeEntry, FinalGrade, ReportCard, ReportSubjectGrade, CompetencyAttainment, LvsResult |
| `learniq-care-and-support` | Support a learner who needs more | LearningPlan, SupportRequest, Trajectory, ConcernReport, GroupPlanSubgroup |
| `learniq-attendance` (exists as part of `AttendanceRecord`'s activity) | Attendance and leerplicht | ExcuseRequest, AttendanceFlag, AttendanceSummary |
| `learniq-work-placement` | BPV | BpvPlacement, WerkprocesAssessment, BpvVisitReport, Praktijkopleider, BpvHourWeek |
| `learniq-parent-contact` | Inform and meet parents | ConferenceRound, ConferenceSignup, ConferenceSlot, ConferenceReport, ReportCardParentNotification, GradeNotification |

The tasks list every schema against one activity; a builder adjusts the grouping where a schema's purpose differs, and writes why.

### D2: Reads logged where the data is sensitive

`logReads: true` for care and support, and for results. Not for enrolment and parent contact, where read logging costs more than it tells.

### D3: A build check, not a promise

The check lists the personal-data field names and fails on any schema that has one and no block. It is the only way the record stays complete after the next twenty schemas.

## Risks

- Retention: most of these schemas declare no archival rule yet. The block then says "not declared", as `Credential`'s does today, which is honest and shows the privacy officer the gap.
