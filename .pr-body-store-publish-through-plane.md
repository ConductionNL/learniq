## Scope

Learniq's course store publishes through OpenRegister's store plane instead of its own HTTP call: `CourseStorePublisher` hands the registry object to `GenericStoreService::publish()` (openregister #4079, landed 84352bae), and `StoreController` asks the plane's `StoreActionAuthorizer::canPublish()` before it builds a package. `CourseStoreUrlGuard`, the objects-API URL and the `IClientService` call leave learniq, so the hydra gate 62 finding from #1043 has nothing left to flag.

Decision D22 (lesson sharing through the store plane). This is the adoption the OpenRegister change describes in `openspec/changes/store-plane-publish/design.md`, section "How learniq adopts this", and the round 2 follow-up "learniq #1043 adopts GenericStoreService::publish".

## What changes

- `CourseStoreDescriptor` names what may leave (`PUBLISH_FIELDS`, exactly the keys `CourseStoreRegistryObject` builds, pinned by a test) and who may send it (`publishGroups` = `ActionAuthService::getAllowedGroups('course-package.share')`, so the matrix an administrator edits stays the one source of truth).
- `CourseStorePublisher` is now four small methods over the plane: `isConfigured()`, `supportsPublish()`, `mayPublish(IUser)`, `publish(array)`.
- `StoreController::publish()` keeps `requireAction('course-package.share')`, then answers 501 `publish_not_supported` on an OpenRegister without the write path and 403 `forbidden` when the plane refuses, both before the sharing gate runs. New statuses: `rate_limited` 429, `store_invalid_response` 502, `not_publishable` 500.
- Duck-typed against the installed OpenRegister: the publish arguments reach `StoreDescriptor` only when it declares them (a named argument it does not know would be fatal), `publish()` and `canPublish()` are probed first, and the authorizer is resolved from the container inside `mayPublish()` so an older OpenRegister cannot take search and install down with it. Every failure to resolve or ask the authorizer is a refusal.
- `ExportRequestView` names the new outcomes in plain words (English and Dutch).
- Stubs follow #4079: `GenericStoreService::publish()` and its outcomes, `StoreDescriptor` publish opt-in, a new `StoreActionAuthorizer` stub, `GenericActionAuthService::getAllowedGroups()`/`can()`; the `SecurityService` stub and its psalm entry are gone with the guard.
- Docs: `docs/user-guide/admin/03-admin-settings.md` says publishing needs an OpenRegister that can publish and names the matrix row that decides who may.

OpenSpec: `openspec/changes/store-publish-through-plane/` (three ADDED requirements in course-management, test plan TC-1 to TC-7). Discovery, contract and migration artifacts skipped: the approach is fixed by the OpenRegister design, learniq exposes no new cross-app interface, and no schema changes.
